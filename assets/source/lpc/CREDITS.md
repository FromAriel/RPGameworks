# LPC art credits

The party characters are built from Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) art, taken from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator. Each file is used under CC-BY 3.0, CC0, OGA-BY 3.0, OGA-BY 3.0+, never a share-alike licence. All of it is used in modified form: layers are composited, recoloured, and reduced to 48-pixel frames by the PixiPPL reducer. Every file's authors and licence are listed in the per-file credits.
