# Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup monster tiles — credits

All images under `dcss/monster/` come from the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup 32 × 32 tile releases: the "Full" pack, plus icons unique to the October 2010 crawl-tiles release. Their artists dedicated them to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (`licences/CC0-1.0.txt`; legal code: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode): no attribution is required, and use does not imply endorsement by the authors. The pack README asks users to credit the Crawl and Crawl Stone Soup artists and say where the tiles came from, which this file does. The tiles grew out of rltiles (http://rltiles.sourceforge.net/), a public domain set for Crawl and NetHack.

Sources:

- https://github.com/crawl/tiles/tree/master/releases
- https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles
- https://opengameart.org/content/dungeon-crawl-32x32-tiles-supplemental

The two pack readmes are kept unchanged in `licences/` (`dcss-full-README.txt`, `crawl-tiles-2010-README.txt`).
