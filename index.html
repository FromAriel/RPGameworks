How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Face someone or something nearby, then press E or Enter to interact. Escape closes messages; Space creates a small spark.

Controller defaults: left stick / D-pad to move, X to interact, B to close, A for sparks. Your custom bindings take priority.

Escape opens this menu during exploration. F2 toggles Debug. Click the play area to play with the panel still visible.

Controller configuration
